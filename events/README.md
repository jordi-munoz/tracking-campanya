# Political events, 29-N 2026 general election campaign

`events_29N.csv` is the machine-readable event list used by the R scripts (one row per event; `outcomes` lists the hypothesised outcome variables, separated by `;`). Compiled on 17 Nov 2026 and updated on 18 and 20 Nov from live blogs and news reports; check the sources before citing. `date_precision = approximate` marks dates not confirmed by a primary news source.

To add an event: give it the next free ID (E32, E33, …; IDs are never renumbered because the reports refer to them), keep rows in date order, and add its label in Catalan, Spanish and English to `event_lab()` in `reports/_common.R`. Whether an event is before, between or during fieldwork, or after the last available block, is not stored in the file: `event_phase()` in `R/fieldwork.R` derives it from `config/fieldwork_blocks.csv`, so later events enter the analysis automatically once a new tracking day covers them.
